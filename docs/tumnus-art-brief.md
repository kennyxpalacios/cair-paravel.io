# Tumnus Art Brief (for image generation)

Paste the block below into an image-capable assistant together with the moodboard references. The finished sheets feed the layered 2D rig described in `01-architecture-stack-testflight.md`, section 2.7.

```text
PROJECT THESIS: TUMNUS FOR CAIR PARAVEL

I'm building Cair Paravel, an iPhone and iPad focus timer for people with ADHD. Focus sessions are "Expeditions" into an enchanted wood, and breaks are "Tea Time". The user is accompanied by Tumnus, the faun from C.S. Lewis's Narnia, who lives on screen as a calm, kind companion. I need your help designing him.

The core idea: Tumnus is what you get when all of my attached references are fused into one faun. He is not a copy of any single reference, not the 2005 film design, and not a flat cartoon mascot. He should feel like a storybook illustration that wandered into a high-end editorial magazine: classical, romantic, and a little luminous.

WHO HE IS
- A slight, gentle, adult faun with kind, slightly anxious eyes. He has the face of someone who would rather make you tea than lecture you.
- Curly dark-brown hair, two small curled horns, pointed ears, warm ruddy skin, and a short pointed beard (true to the book; show one concept without the beard too).
- Human from the waist up and goat from the waist down: shaggy legs with glossy dark fur, cloven hooves, and a long tail he sometimes carries over his arm.
- Costume: a long red wool scarf with an embroidered folk-art border, a simple waistcoat, an umbrella, and brown-paper parcels tied with string.

STYLE RECIPE (HOW THE REFERENCES COMBINE)
- Linework (the Fellowship poster, the Rivendell bridge, the pastel sci-fi landscape): confident ink contours with varying weight that go dry and broken at the ends. Never a uniform cartoon outline.
- Color (the lamppost watercolor and the three Aslan paintings): transparent watercolor washes over visible paper grain. Umber and sienna fur, a vermilion scarf, an indigo umbrella that turns amber where lamplight shines through it.
- Ornament (the folk-pattern lion): the embroidered border on the scarf hem and waistcoat.
- Light (the lamppost, the hiker in the magenta beam, the pastel orbs): a warm amber key light from above, a cold snow-blue fill, a thin neon rim light (magenta or cyan) along his silhouette, and a soft chromatic halo at his edges.
- Texture (the blue Kraken engravings): fine engraved cross-hatching only in the deepest shadows.
- Presence (the glowing, morphing light-bulb video): a soft luminous aura behind him that could swell or dim with his mood.
- Restraint (the FANTASY and QUEST posters): a clean silhouette and strong negative space. This is a calm app for ADHD brains, so he must read at a glance and never look busy or noisy.

WHAT I NEED (one step at a time; wait for my feedback before moving on)
1. Character concepts: three different takes on Tumnus, full body and standing, on a plain background. Vary proportions and costume details, and keep the style recipe.
2. Turnaround: front, three-quarter, side, and back views of the chosen design, at the same scale, on a plain background.
3. Expression sheet (head only): calm, focused, curious, gently concerned, cozy and content, quietly proud.
4. Mood poses, full body, each first on a plain background and then in a scene:
   - Waiting: under a lamppost, umbrella up, a warm amber pool of light, falling snow.
   - Expedition: walking ahead through a deep forest, using the umbrella as a walking stick and glancing back over his shoulder. Cool forest light with a magenta or cyan rim.
   - Wander: turned toward the viewer, brows lifted, one hand held out. Softly concerned, never scolding.
   - Tea Time: seated with a steaming teacup, scarf loosened, a hearth-amber glow.
   - Milestone: a small bow with the tip of his umbrella, light blooming softly behind him.
5. Parts sheet for animation: the chosen design broken apart into separate pieces with clear gaps between them, laid out on one sheet. Pieces: head (hair, ears, horns), eyes and brows, torso with waistcoat, scarf body, two scarf tails, left arm, right arm, umbrella, parcels, teacup, left leg, right leg, tail. Use the same lighting and scale as the turnaround. These pieces will be traced into layers for an animation rig.

RULES FOR EVERY IMAGE
- No text, letters, logos, signatures, or watermarks.
- Always the same character: once a design is chosen, lock the face, horns, scarf pattern, and colors.
- For sheets, use a transparent background if you can, otherwise a flat warm off-white (around #F2EDE4) so he can be cut out cleanly.
- Keep the full figure in frame with generous margins and nothing cropped.
- Use portrait 2:3 for single figures and landscape 3:2 for sheets, at the highest resolution available.
- Keep the design original: use the references for mood, palette, light, and technique, not to copy any existing character design.

Start with step 1.
```
